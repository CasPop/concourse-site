Concourse privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-12.

Concourse is a game about running an airport gate, made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy says what the game collects, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. Questions: stephenstidham@yahoo.com.

The short version

  • We collect what the game needs to run your account and your gate, and play stats that you can turn off.
  • No ads. We never sell or rent your data. There is no third-party analytics or advertising code in the game.
  • You can delete your account at any time in the game, or ask us on the web.
  • The game has a chat room, the Lounge. What you write there can be read by every player.

What we collect

Your account

  • An account id. The game creates one when you tap Play. You start as a guest: the account has no name and no email.
  • Sign in with Apple (if you save your gate this way): Apple gives us an id for you that only this game sees, and the email address you chose to share, which can be Apple's private relay address. The name Apple offers is kept on your phone and never sent to our server.
  • Email sign-in (if you use it): your email address and a password. Our host's sign-in service stores the password only as a secure hash.
  • Sign-in records: when you signed in, and the tokens that keep you signed in.
  • Sign in with Google (on Android, if you save your gate this way): Google gives us an id for you that only this game sees, and your Google account's email address. The name Google offers is kept on your phone and never sent to our server.

Your player profile

  • A callsign and two colours. The game makes up a callsign for you (like SKYLINE BLUE) and picks two colours. You can change them. Other players see them.
  • Roughly when you last played, updated at most every five minutes, so other players can see an active gate.

Your game

  • Your gate, planes, flights, equipment, crew, tech, rounds and results, and your in-game money and points (Cash, Parts, Permits, Goodwill, Miles).
  • The decor and props you own and where you placed them, the pieces on your wishlist, and the pieces you saw on other players' gates.

Things between players

  • Visits: who visited whose gate, and when.
  • Kudos: who gave kudos to whom, by day.
  • Thank-you stamps: when you turn an inbound plane that wears another player's callsign, a stamp with your callsign goes in their book.
  • Gifts: who sent what to whom, the Miles it cost, the stamp chosen, and whether it was signed.
  • Crossed paths: whose inbound planes you turned, so the game knows who you can send a gift to.

The Lounge (chat)

The Lounge is one chat room for every player.

  • What you write: each line you send (up to 200 characters) or wave you give, with your callsign as it read at that moment and the time. We keep the times of your recent lines to stop flooding. Some words are masked with asterisks by a filter before a line is stored; the line is never rejected for them.
  • Announcements: when you finish your first round, reach a new equipment era, earn a top-rank capstone or add a plane to your fleet, the Lounge shows a short card with your callsign. When several players do the same thing close together, one card names them together.
  • Mutes: the players you have muted, so their lines stay out of your Lounge. They are not told.
  • Reports: if you report a line, we keep which line you reported, the reason you typed (if any) and the time. While the game is in testing, the reported line, the callsign of the player who wrote it and your reason are also posted to the feedback channel of our Discord server, where we and the other testers in that server can read them. Your name is not posted with it.
  • Moderation: we can hide a line from the Lounge and stop an account from writing there for a time. We keep a note of who is stopped, until when, and why.

If "Share anonymous play stats" is on, the stats count that you opened the Lounge, sent a line or waved. They never hold what you wrote.

Do not put anything in the Lounge that you would not want every player to read: no real names, addresses, phone numbers or passwords.

Play stats

If "Share anonymous play stats" is on in Settings (it is on until you turn it off), the game sends us what you do in it: the screens and sheets you open, the rounds and mini-games you play and how they went, the upgrades you buy, the Shop pieces you look at, buy or give, the steps of the first session, and how you are signed in (guest, Apple or email). Each event carries a random session id, the app version and build, the platform (iOS or Android), the kind of device (phone, tablet, desktop, web), the operating system's name and the screen size.

The stats are filed under your account id. They hold no name, no email, no device or advertising identifier, no location, and no IP address. Turning the setting off deletes the stats still waiting on your phone and stops every send.

Bug reports

If you tap Report a bug (in Settings, or with a three-finger tap during play) and then Send, the game sends us a picture of the game's screen at that moment, the note you typed (if any), where you were in the game (the screen, the open sheet, the round and mini-game you were in), the last minute of what you did in the game (the same kind of record as the play stats), the last 100 lines of the game's technical log, and the app version, build, platform and kind of device. Nothing is sent until you tap Send. The screenshot shows only the game, never anything else on your phone.

While the game is in testing, each bug report you send is also posted to the feedback channel of our Discord server, where we and the other testers in that server can read it: the picture, your note, where you were in the game, the build and platform, and your callsign. Error reports the game sends by itself are posted there too, with only the error's first line, where you were in the game, the build and platform, and your callsign. The last minute of what you did and the game's log are never posted there. A short line about each bug report you send, your callsign and the first words of your note, also appears on a pinned board in that channel while the problem is open and for a week after it is fixed.

If the game hits a programming error while "Share anonymous play stats" is on, it sends us an error report by itself: the error's text, where you were in the game, the last minute of what you did and the game's log, never a screenshot or a note. At most ten a launch, each error once. Turn the play stats off and it sends none.

Bug and error reports are filed under your account id, used only to find and fix problems, and deleted from our server with your account. The copy posted in Discord, and its line on the pinned board, is deleted with your account too. Reports posted before 2026-11-12 were sent before we could do that; ask us (see Contact) and we will delete those.

On your phone only

Your sound, haptics and play stats settings, your first-session progress and your sign-in session stay on your phone, and so do the last minute of play and the game's log that a bug report would carry, until you send one. If you allow notifications (iOS), the game schedules a reminder on your phone for when a plane lands; nothing about it is sent to us or to a push service.

What our host records

Supabase, which runs our server, keeps short-lived technical logs of requests (such as the IP address, the time and the address requested) to run and protect the service, and its sign-in service keeps a log of sign-in events that includes the IP address.

The web deletion form

If you use the form on our deletion page, we keep the callsign you give, how you played, the email address if you give one, and a one-way hash of your network address (used only to stop repeated requests). It is deleted with your account.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, advertising id or device identifiers. No payment details. Nothing in the game costs money today: the Get Miles sheet shows its packs but charges nothing. If purchases arrive, Apple or Google will take the payment and we will receive only a record of what was bought and when, never your card details, and this policy will say so first.

How we use it

  • To run the game: your account, your gate and its saves, and the parts you share with other players.
  • To improve the game: the play stats show how the game is played and where players stop. They are not used for ads or profiling and are not combined with data from anywhere else.
  • To keep it safe: rate limits and stopping abuse, including the Lounge's word filter, reports and mutes.

We do not sell your data, rent it, or share it for advertising.

What other players can see

  • Your callsign, your two colours, and roughly when you last played.
  • Your gate as you decorated it: decor, props, equipment levels, planes and their liveries and the parts fitted to them, and this week's kudos, visits and arrivals.
  • When you visit a gate, land at one at the end of a round, give kudos or turn an inbound plane wearing a player's callsign, your callsign and colours show in that player's book.
  • While you have played in the last week, your callsign and colours can appear on other players' inbound planes.
  • A signed gift shows your callsign to the player who gets it. An unsigned one does not.
  • Everything you write in the Lounge, with your callsign, for as long as it is kept (see How long we keep it).
  • The Lounge's announcement cards that name your callsign.
  • A bug report you send, which the testers in our Discord server can read (see Bug reports).

Other players' games receive your account id (a random code) so they can open your gate. It is never shown and says nothing about you. Other players never see your email or how you sign in.

Who handles your data for us

  • Supabase hosts our database, our server code and sign-in, in the United States (Ohio). It handles the data only to run the service for us.
  • Apple distributes the game (App Store and TestFlight) and runs Sign in with Apple, under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Google distributes the game on Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Discord hosts our testers' server; bug reports you send, and Lounge lines that a player reports, are posted to a channel there, under Discord's own privacy policy.
  • Our privacy and deletion web pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google for them.

Nobody else.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, your game and your play stats: while your account exists. We may delete old play stats sooner.
  • Bug and error reports on our server: while your account exists, and we may delete them sooner once the problem is fixed.
  • Bug and error reports posted in our Discord channel, and their lines on its pinned board: until we clear the channel or your account is deleted. Reports posted there before 2026-11-12: until we clear the channel or you ask us to remove yours.
  • Lounge lines and announcement cards: 30 days, or until 500 newer lines have been written, whichever comes first. A line we hide stays on our server for the same time, out of sight.
  • Your mutes: until you undo them or your account is deleted.
  • Lounge reports on our server, and their copies in our Discord channel: until we clear them, or until the account of the player who reported or of the player who wrote the line is deleted.
  • A guest account lives on one phone. If you uninstall the game or lose the phone, you cannot reach the account again, but it stays on our server until you ask us to delete it (use the web form below and give your callsign).
  • Our host's technical logs: a short time, on its own schedule.
  • Our host's routine backups can hold deleted data until they expire. We never restore a deleted account from them.

Deleting your account

In the game: Airline tab, Settings, Delete my account, then hold the red button for two seconds. It happens at once and cannot be undone. We delete your account, your profile, your gate and everything in it, everything you own, your wishlist, your visits, kudos, stamps and gifts, your play stats, your bug reports and their screenshots, and everything of yours in the Lounge: your lines, your name on announcement cards, your mutes, and reports you made or that were made about your lines, with their copies in our Discord channel. Reports already posted in our Discord channel are deleted from it too, and leave its pinned board; reports posted there before 2026-11-12 we delete when you ask. The game then returns to its first screen.

For other players: your visits, kudos and stamps leave their books. A gift you sent that they have not opened yet stays theirs, unsigned. A gift someone sent you that you had not opened goes back to them as the Miles they paid.

On the web: if you cannot open the game, use the form at https://caspop.github.io/concourse-site/delete. We delete the account by hand within 30 days.

If you signed in with Apple you can also stop using your Apple ID with Concourse in your Apple ID settings. That signs the game out of Apple but does not delete your game data; deleting the account does.

Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can turn the play stats off at any time. You can change your callsign and colours in the game. In the Lounge you can mute any player and report any line. We run the game on the basis of providing it to you, and collect play stats on the basis of our interest in improving it, which you can switch off. If you are in the EU or the UK you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Concourse is for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will delete it.

Security

The game talks to our server only over HTTPS. The database is closed to the game itself: every read and write goes through our server code, which checks who is asking.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date at the top, and for a change that matters we say so in the game.
