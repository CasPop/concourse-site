Delete your Concourse account

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Concourse and go to the Airline tab.
  2. In Settings, tap Delete my account.
  3. Read the warning, then hold the red button for two seconds.

This deletes your account at once and for good: your gate, planes, Miles and everything you own, your callsign, your visits, kudos and gifts, and your play stats. It cannot be undone. The game then returns to its first screen.

Ask us to delete it

If you cannot open the game any more (you uninstalled it, or a guest account was on a phone you no longer have), send this form. We delete the account by hand within 30 days. We use what you send only to find and delete your account, and we delete the request with it.
