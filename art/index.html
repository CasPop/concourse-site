How this works
  • You only need a phone. Nothing to install, no account.
  • Your link is yours. Please do not pass it on: it is how the desk knows the piece is from you.
  • Send your own work only: something you drew, painted or made yourself.
  • You can send as many as you like.
  • Nothing goes in the game until Stephen has looked at it with you.
  • Your name goes on it, in the game and in the Shop.
  • Stephen sets it up in the game, picks its price, and sends you a picture of it on the gate.

    A few more

    • Tap the piece on your screen to focus, then hold still.
    • Use the normal camera at 1x: no zoom, no filter, no portrait mode.
    • Curled paper? Flatten it under a book first.
    • Dark pen or marker reads best. With pencil, press firmly.
    • Wet or glossy paint shines. Turn the piece, or move, until the bright patch is gone.
    • Take it out from behind glass.
    • A big canvas: lean it against a wall, stand back, and hold the phone level with its middle.

    Choose the picture file from your phone, tablet or computer. PNG, JPEG, WebP, GIF and most picture files work.

    A screenshot works too. Open your picture so it fills the screen, take a screenshot, and choose the screenshot here. You can crop it on the next step.

    It looks wrong?
    • The colours look pale or washed out. Switch to Painted or coloured at the top.
    • It is dark or grey. Move Brightness (or, on paper, Make the paper whiter), or take it again by a window.
    • The edges are cut off. Under Fit, pick Show the whole piece.
    • It is sideways. Tap Turn a quarter.
    • The wrong part is picked. Drag the four dots onto the corners of your piece.
    • The table shows at the edge. Drag the dots in a little.
    • There is a bright patch. That is a shine. Take it again: turn the piece or step to the side until it goes.
    • It is blurry. Take it again: tap the piece on your screen to focus, and hold still.
    • It will not send. Check your signal first. If the page says the file is too big, tap Send again: it makes it smaller.
