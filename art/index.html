Opening the art desk...

What did you draw?

Draw it once on paper, take a photo, and see it in the game before you send it. Your name goes on it in the game and in the shop.

For a clean photo

  • The whole sheet in the photo, on a darker table, nothing on top of it.
  • Even daylight. No flash, and no shadow of your phone across the paper.
  • Hold the phone flat above the sheet. A little tilt is fine: the desk straightens it.

, in the game

Shown the size a player sees it on a phone.

The paper, the fit, the light, the colours

The paper

Drag the four dots to frame exactly what is kept; the yellow one is the top left of your drawing. Left alone, the tool trims the blank paper for you and the blue dashes show what it keeps. Dragging a dot turns that trim off.
